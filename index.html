Sci-Image — User Guide & Publication Standards

Creating Publication-Quality Scientific Figures

Sci-Image is an open-source vector studio designed specifically for scientific researchers, chemists, and biologists.

  1. Select your target journal format: In the right sidebar under Templates, pick standard single-column (85 mm), double-column (170 mm), or square graphical abstract.
  2. Insert biological assets & chemical structures: Use the left toolbar to drop pre-made bioicons, enter chemical SMILES or PubChem compound names, or generate lipid membranes and DNA helices.
  3. Use accessible palettes: Switch to the Palettes tab to apply Nature/Science compliant color-blind friendly palettes (Okabe-Ito, Viridis, Tol).
  4. Export at 300+ DPI: Click Export in the top header and select Journal Print PNG (300 DPI) or Scalable Vector (SVG) for submission.

SMILES & Molecular Cheminformatics

You can generate chemical structures in three ways:

  • SMILES Strings: Paste any SMILES string into the Molecule generator (e.g. CC(=O)OC1=CC=CC=C1C(=O)O for Aspirin or CN1C=NC2=C1C(=O)N(C(=O)N2C)C for Caffeine).
  • PubChem Compound Search: Type common chemical names such as "Dopamine", "Penicillin", "ATP", or "Cholesterol" and click Search PubChem.
  • Curated Library: Choose from over 30 essential bioactives, amino acids, nucleotides, and solvents in the Molecule drawer.
  • Editable Vectors: All generated molecules are normal vector groups. Click Ungroup in the Style panel to edit individual bonds, heteroatom labels, or colors.

Cellular Membranes, DNA & Proteins

  • Membrane Path Brush: Trace custom curves across the canvas; dual phospholipid leaflets are generated automatically perpendicular to the path.
  • Liposomes & Vesicles: Generate circular lipid vesicles or micelles with customizable radius and lipid density.
  • DNA Double-Helix: Generates 3D-effect antiparallel strands with color-coded 5' and 3' backbones and base-pair rungs.
  • Protein Visualizers: Parametric alpha-helices, pleated beta-sheets, and Y-shaped Immunoglobulin G (IgG) antibodies with Fab and Fc domains.

Scientific Vector Icon & Workflow Style Guide

To ensure visual consistency and publication elegance across all figures, blueprints, and contributed vector icons, Sci-Image enforces standard vector conventions:

1. Icon Geometry & Stroke Standards

  • Coordinate ViewBox: Exactly 0 0 64 64. All paths must fit cleanly within a 4px inner margin (active drawing area 4 to 60px).
  • Primary Stroke Weight: 2.0px to 2.5px for outer boundaries and primary instrument silhouettes; 1.5px to 1.8px for internal features.
  • Line Caps & Joins: Always specify stroke-linecap="round" and stroke-linejoin="round" to avoid sharp miter spikes.
  • Subtle Fills: 8% to 15% opacity fills (e.g. rgba(14,165,233,0.12)) provide volume without visual clutter.

3. Flow Steps & Sticky Connectors

  • Step Cards: Standard 280 × 170px container with 8px corner radius, top accent bar, and numbered circular badge.
  • Decision Branches: Rhombus diamond with Yes/No connecting branches pointing to downstream resolution steps.
  • Sticky Routing: When moving any card on canvas, attached flow arrows automatically recalculate and track the nearest anchor point in real time.

Academic Journal Specifications (Nature, Science, Cell)

  • Column Dimensions:
    • Single column: 85 mm (approx. 1004 px at 300 DPI)
    • 1.5 column: 120 mm (approx. 1417 px at 300 DPI)
    • Double column: 170 mm (approx. 2008 px at 300 DPI)
  • Resolution: High-DPI raster images must be at least 300 DPI for color figures, or 600–1200 DPI for line art.
  • Typography: Use clean sans-serif typefaces (IBM Plex Sans, Helvetica, Arial) between 6 pt and 10 pt. Labels must remain legible at final printed scale.
  • Color Accessibility: Avoid rainbow or red-green palettes. Use Okabe-Ito or Viridis palettes so figures can be interpreted by individuals with deuteranopia or protanopia.
